Magna Carta Aventuria

Discover Aventuria like never before with the new DSA5 Atlas module.

Bring the Magna Carta Aventurica to your virtual table as a living map: search locations in seconds, jump directly to points of interest, and keep your group oriented with route planning and guided travel. Regional overlays help you read the land at a glance, while handy tools make it easy to pan to the party and stay focused on the story instead of the bookkeeping.

Highlights you can look forward to:

  • Fast location search across the map with quick navigation
  • Route planning with waypoints, travel options, and step-by-step or automatic travel
  • Region overlays (political regions, subregions, biomes) for instant context
  • Integrated weather support for extra atmosphere and immersion
  • GM-friendly configuration and smooth table workflow

Whether you’re preparing a campaign or improvising on the fly, the DSA5 Atlas turns your world map into an interactive adventure tool.
