A Goblin more or less

A new FREE adventure soon available. It marks the start of the upcoming campaign "The nine-fingered claw" (also known as Theaterknights) coming to Foundry VTT soon.

Festum, the prosperous merchant city and capital of the Bornland, is home to more than 30,000 humans. For many years, the city has also been home to a growing population of goblins. Called “red pelts” in other parts of Aventuria, goblins were granted citizenship in Festum a number of years ago when they saved the city from a serious plague of rats. Festum goblins now work as day laborers, street sweepers, and rat catchers, and perform their jobs with pride even if many human residents find these occupations disgusting. To some, a goblin more or less makes little difference, but even red pelts have families who worry about them. When a goblin is found murdered and the City Guard fails to find any leads, an upstanding but overworked sergeant recruits the heroes to help with the investigation. Soon, more bodies turn up, and the heroes find themselves racing against time to save Festum’s goblins from a fiendish serial killer!

The Thorwal drum

A new FREE adventure soon available. It marks the start of the upcoming campaign "The nine-fingered claw" (also known as Theaterknights) coming to Foundry VTT soon.

The Middenrealm marches to war against Lord Commander Helme Haffax, but Festum, the trade capital on the Sea of Pearls, has its own problems. The city’s once-cherished tradition of the Atmaskottje Parade has become a serious issue of contention, and the population is divided. Many would agree that the Thorwal Drum, a historic relic crafted from human skin, is what angers Festumers the most. Left unchecked, this growing rift could destroy the city’s unique, cosmopolitan atmosphere. It wasn’t so long ago that Festum’s populations of goblins, Thorwalers, Norbards, Maraskans, and native citizens all got along well. Now, a simple parade could spark a riot that leaves the city in ruins. Faced with saboteurs, enraged citizens, and political powerplays, Guard Captain Timpski is betting that intelligent heroes can turn the tide and soothe the waters. They will need every ounce of tact, ingenuity, cunning, and muscle to resist the bleak wind that moves through the streets of the Bornish metropolis. Peace is threatened, and in the end, only one thing is certain—the future of the entire city is linked to the infamous Thorwal Drum....

New Modules

The Nine-Fingered Claw

The first part of the campaign The Nine-Fingered Claw includes the three adventures The White Lake, The Blue Book, and The Black Forest. These are supplemented by the new scenario The Earthen Tower, the Heldenwerk adventures The Thorwaler Drum and One More Goblin... or Less, as well as additional expansions.

The White Lake: The Thorwaler Drum, a symbol of the pride and patriotism of the people of Festum, disappeared from the capital of the Bornland in the spring. As the first snowflakes begin to fall, a band of armed men led by Captain of the Guard Timpski sets out to recover the drum—by force if necessary.

The Blue Book: The heroes are tasked by the Temple of Efferd with escorting Thora Fataburuq's final gift—the legendary legacy of the Theater Knights—from Neersand to Hinterbruch. But what begins as a routine escort mission soon turns into a rescue expedition into the ruins of Pilkamm, culminating in a bloody crime orchestrated by power-hungry cultists.

The Black Forest: "The Bravest." That is the sole requirement set by the Temple of Rondra in Firunen for whoever wishes to transport a shipment of Uhdenberg steel to the renowned merchant city. On the bleaching grounds of Irberod, beneath the shadow of the soot-blackened ironworks, followers of Rondra from many different backgrounds gather to compete in a contest that will determine who among them is truly the bravest.
