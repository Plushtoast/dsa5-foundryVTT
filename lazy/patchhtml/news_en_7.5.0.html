News (03/15/2026)

Sale in the F-Shop

From March 16 through March 29 we will run a staggered sale for DSA VTT products in the German F-Shop. Entry products will be discounted by 70%, while everything else will be discounted by 40%. If you want to get started with DSA Virtual Tabletop or fill gaps in your library, this is the perfect opportunity.

Please note that this sale will only be available in the German shop. While modules themselves are bilingual when translations already exist, you should check first whether a given module has been translated already, for example by browsing the English modules in the shop tab.

Foundry V14

Foundry Version 14 is almost finished by the Foundry team and brings a few more features that we are already tinkering with.

Please do not update to V14 immediately without first confirming that DSA is ready for it. DSA will most likely not be ready at the same time as V14 or shortly after. The reason is, once again, extensive required refactoring work combined with a few new DSA enhancements.

We ask for your understanding so we can make that transition as stable and clean as possible.
